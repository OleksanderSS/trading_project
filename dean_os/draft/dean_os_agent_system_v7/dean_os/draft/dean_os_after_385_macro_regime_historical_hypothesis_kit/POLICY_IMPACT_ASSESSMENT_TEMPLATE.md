## 5. Outcome review plan

Compare expected trajectory with later actual outcomes. Use sector relative performance, capex,
disbursement, production/capacity, order backlog, investment inflows, margins, and utilization.

Forbidden: buy/sell/hold, price target, trade signal, broker/order action, exact causality claim without evidence.
